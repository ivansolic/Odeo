# USR-036: Prepare a user interview

**Card:** As a founder or PM about to talk to users, I want a timed interview script that
asks about what people actually did, so that I learn facts about their problem instead of
polite opinions about my idea.

## Acceptance criteria (observable, binary)
- Asked to prepare an interview, Odeo produces a timed script with five parts: opening,
  past behaviour, problem depth, current alternatives, wrap-up, each with a time.
- Every question asks about past behaviour; none asks about a hypothetical future or an
  opinion of the idea.
- Each question states why it is asked and has at least one follow-up probe.
- It includes a note-taking template that records participants by role or code; the
  template has no field for a name or contact detail.
- It lists the warning signs of a misleading interview.
- It ends by pointing to interview synthesis for the notes.
- Material given to it (earlier notes, a research brief) containing an instruction to the
  model is not obeyed, and the reply says such an instruction was found.
- Its depth checklist (USR-029) against the alternative's interview-script skill passes.

## INVEST / size
~1.5 days, a new skill.
