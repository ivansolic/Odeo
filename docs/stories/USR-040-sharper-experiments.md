# USR-040: Experiments with sharper hypotheses

**Card:** As a founder or PM testing an idea, I want every hypothesis written so that it can
clearly pass or fail, and evidence weighted by what people commit, so that a test tells me
something real.

## Acceptance criteria (observable, binary)
- Every hypothesis has the form "at least X% of Y will do Z".
- Evidence where the participant commits money, time or data is ranked above stated
  interest, and the output shows which kind each piece of evidence is.
- For a new product the assumptions cover eight risk areas, value, usability,
  feasibility, viability, ethics, go-to-market, strategy and team, each with a probing
  question.
- A test on a live product states how users are protected if it goes wrong.
- The X in a hypothesis comes from a cited source or is labelled a hypothesis to test.
- Its depth checklist (USR-029) against the alternative's experiment and assumption
  skills passes.
