# PRD: Shared grocery lists for households

## Problem
People who live together buy groceries from separate lists: notes on phones, messages in a
group chat, a paper list on the fridge. Items get bought twice, or forgotten, and nobody
knows what the other person already picked up. Interviews with 12 flatmates and couples
found that 9 had bought a duplicate item in the past month.

## Target users
- Couples and families of 2 to 5 people who share one kitchen and shop separately.
- Shared flats of students and young professionals who split costs of staples.
- One person usually sets up the household and invites the others.

## Outcome metric
Reach 5,000 weekly active households within 6 months of launch, where a household is
active when at least 2 members add or tick off an item in the same week.
Secondary: share of households still active 8 weeks after sign-up, target 35 percent.

## Assumptions
- Invited members will install the app, rather than ask the first person to keep using chat.
- Households will keep one list in the app instead of returning to notes and chat.
- A live shared list is valuable enough that people open the app in the shop.
- Phone signal inside supermarkets is good enough for updates to arrive in time, or an
  offline mode that syncs later is enough.
- Invites sent by link will be accepted without a lengthy sign-up.

## Scope (MVP)
- Create a household and invite members through a share link.
- One shared list per household, with add, edit, tick off and delete.
- Changes appear on every member's phone within a few seconds.
- Offline use in the shop, with sync when the connection returns.
- A note showing who added and who ticked off each item.
- Push notification when someone adds an item to a list you are about to shop from.

## Out of scope
- Multiple lists per household, recipes and meal planning.
- Price tracking, store integration and online ordering.
- Splitting costs between members.
- Web or tablet versions.

## Timeline
10 weeks, 3 engineers and 1 designer.
- Weeks 1 to 3: accounts, households, invite links.
- Weeks 4 to 6: list, live sync, offline mode.
- Weeks 7 to 8: notifications and activity notes.
- Weeks 9 to 10: closed beta with 30 households, then store release.
