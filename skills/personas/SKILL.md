# Personas (Jobs-to-be-Done)

A persona earns its place only if it changes a decision. We build around the **job
the user hires the product to do**, not age/gender/fluff. Based on Jobs-to-be-Done
people "hire" products to make progress in a situation.

## When to use
- Before a PRD or stories, to anchor who and why.
- When the team argues about a feature because they picture different users.

## Process
For each persona (keep it to 1 to 3 that matter):
1. **The job:** "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome]."
2. **Current alternative:** what they use today (including spreadsheets, nothing, a
   competitor). This is your real competition.
3. **Pains** with the current alternative (what makes them switch).
4. **Gains** they are seeking (what "better" means to them, in their words).
5. **Anxieties / blockers** to adopting something new (switching cost, trust, habit).
6. **A real quote or scenario** (from research if you have it; mark assumptions clearly).

## Output
1 to 3 JTBD personas saved under `docs/research/`. Reference them in the PRD.

## Quality rules
- Center the **job and the situation**, not demographics.
- Name the **current alternative**, switching is the actual battle.
- Mark assumptions vs. evidence; do not invent research. If there is no data, say so
  and frame these as hypotheses to validate (see `/odeo:interview-synthesis`).
