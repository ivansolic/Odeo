# Market segments

"Everyone" is not a market. This finds the **specific beachhead** you can win first
and the path outward, and sizes it honestly. Beachhead thinking (win a narrow segment
first, then expand) plus a grounded TAM/SAM/SOM.

## When to use
- Early strategy/PRD work; when the target is vaguely "all users"; before GTM.

## Process
1. **Segment** by what actually changes behavior/need (job, use-case, company size,
   urgency), not just demographics or industry labels.
2. **Pick a beachhead:** the narrowest segment where the pain is sharpest, you can reach
   them, and a win is referenceable. Justify why this one first.
3. **Define the ICP:** the characteristics of an ideal customer in that beachhead (so you
   can say no to bad-fit users).
4. **Size it soberly:** TAM (whole market), SAM (who you could serve), SOM (who you can
   realistically win near-term). Show the assumptions; bottom-up beats a top-down "1% of a huge number."
5. **Expansion path:** the adjacent segments you move to after the beachhead.

## Output
Segments + a chosen beachhead + ICP + TAM/SAM/SOM with assumptions, saved under
`docs/research/`. Feeds `/odeo:strategy`, `/odeo:prd`, `/odeo:gtm-plan`.

## Quality rules
- Narrow beachhead over broad "everyone"; focus is the strategy.
- Bottom-up sizing with visible assumptions; distrust "1% of a billion."
- Segment on behavior/need, not labels. An ICP must let you reject bad-fit users.
