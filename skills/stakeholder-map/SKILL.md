# Stakeholder map

Know who can make or break the work, and engage each appropriately. Based on Mendelow's
Power/Interest grid.

## When to use
- Before a launch or a significant decision; in any effort touching multiple teams or leaders.

## Process
1. **List stakeholders** (people/groups who influence or are affected by the work).
2. **Place each on Power (high/low) x Interest (high/low):**
   - High power, high interest -> **manage closely** (involve, co-decide)
   - High power, low interest -> **keep satisfied** (brief, don't overload)
   - Low power, high interest -> **keep informed** (updates; they're allies/users)
   - Low power, low interest -> **monitor** (light touch)
3. For each key one: what they care about, likely stance (**champion / neutral / blocker**), and the one thing they need from you.
4. **Communication plan:** who gets what, how often, in what format.

## Output
A power/interest grid + a short comms plan saved to `docs/`. Useful input to `/odeo:go-to-market` and `/odeo:plan`.

## Example
```
Eng lead     high power / high interest -> manage closely (co-decide scope)
Finance      high power / low interest  -> keep satisfied (one-line cost summary)
Early users  low power  / high interest -> keep informed (changelog, beta invites)
```

## Quality rules
- Place by power AND interest; treating everyone the same wastes effort and misses blockers.
- Name likely **blockers** early and what would move them, don't discover them at launch.
- A map without a comms plan is half-done.
