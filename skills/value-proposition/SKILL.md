# Value proposition

A value prop is the **demonstrable fit** between the customer's jobs/pains/gains and your
product's pain-relievers and gain-creators. Based on the Value Proposition
Canvas, paired with Jobs-to-be-Done.

## When to use
- Before a PRD or `/positioning`; when you can't crisply say why someone should care.

## Process
1. **Customer profile** (pull from `/personas` if done):
   - **Jobs:** what they're trying to get done.
   - **Pains:** frustrations, risks, obstacles with the current way.
   - **Gains:** outcomes and benefits they want.
2. **Value map:**
   - **Products/features** you offer.
   - **Pain relievers:** how each kills a specific pain.
   - **Gain creators:** how each produces a specific gain.
3. **Check fit:** line up relievers/creators against the *ranked* pains/gains. Address the
   top pains, not the easy ones. Drop features that relieve nothing that matters.
4. **Draft the one-liner:** "We help [segment] who [job] by [pain relief/gain] unlike
   [alternative]." Make it falsifiable, not fluff.

## Output
A value-prop canvas + a sharp one-liner, saved under `docs/`. Feeds `/prd`, `/positioning`, `/gtm-plan`.

## Quality rules
- Map to the **ranked** pains/gains; relieving a trivial pain is not value.
- Tie every claimed benefit to a real pain or gain; cut features that fit nothing.
- "Unlike [alternative]" must be true and specific, no fluff adjectives.
